# Legal and tax launch review

- Confirm legal entity, contracting party, addresses, and support contacts.
- Approve Terms, Privacy, Retention, refund, cancellation, and acceptable-use
  wording for each launch country.
- Map controller/processor roles and sign required data-processing terms.
- Approve subprocessors, international transfers, breach workflow, and data
  subject request workflow.
- Confirm POS receipt, invoice, fiscalisation, record-retention, and consumer
  requirements by business niche and location.
- Confirm subscription sales tax/VAT/GST, place-of-supply, invoice wording,
  registration thresholds, withholding, currency, and refund treatment.
- Confirm Safepay merchant terms, recurring-payment consent, webhooks, disputes,
  chargebacks, and failed-payment communications.
- Run accessibility, app-store privacy disclosure, and age-rating review.
- Publish final policies over HTTPS and record non-draft versions.
- Record reviewer names, dates, jurisdictions, and `Approved` status in
  FlexiPOS SaaS Settings. Keep evidence outside the application repository.
