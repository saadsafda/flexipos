## Scope and roles

This policy describes Sprout account, subscription, support, operational, and
product data. For POS customer and transaction data, the subscribing business
is normally the controller and Sprout acts as its processor; confirm these
roles for each jurisdiction.

## Data collected

- Account and business profile data.
- Staff identity, role, device, and security metadata.
- POS catalog, customer, transaction, shift, stock, and audit records selected
  by the subscribing business.
- Subscription identifiers, invoice metadata, and hosted-payment results. Raw
  card numbers and security codes are not stored by Sprout.
- Support requests, delivery logs, service health, and privacy-minimised
  aggregate operational metrics.

## Offline storage and synchronization

Operational data and queued changes are stored on the POS device so sales can
continue offline. They synchronize after connectivity returns. Device storage
must be protected by the business.
